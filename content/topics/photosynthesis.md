# Photosynthesis: the essentials

Plants, algae and some bacteria make their own food using **light energy**.

## The overall equation

> 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂

## Two stages

1. **Light-dependent reactions** happen in the *thylakoid membranes*. Light splits water, which releases oxygen and makes ATP and NADPH.
2. **The Calvin cycle** happens in the *stroma*. It uses ATP and NADPH to turn CO₂ into sugar.

| Stage          | Where        | Inputs              | Outputs           |
|----------------|--------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Light reactions | Thylakoids  | Light, H₂O          | O₂, ATP, NADPH    |
| Calvin cycle   | Stroma       | CO₂, ATP, NADPH     | Glucose (sugar)   |
